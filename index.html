Who we are

Softregen is two engineers. We have written software professionally since 1994 and 1997 — thirty-two and twenty-nine years — across enterprise systems, distributed architecture and regulated environments.

That matters here more than it usually does. Compliance documentation is not a place for software that mostly works. If a system tells a company it is outside the scope of a regulation and it is not, the company carries that mistake to a regulator. So we build the way that problem deserves: every change to every record is stored as an event, so the full history of how a conclusion was reached is always recoverable — not just the conclusion.

Why the EU AI Act

The AI Act entered into force in 2024 and applies across the Union. It asks organisations that build or deploy AI systems to classify what they have, document it, and keep that documentation current. Most of the burden lands on people who are not lawyers and did not choose this work.

The regulation is long, cross-referenced, and published in 24 languages that are all equally authentic. Reading it is a real job. Producing the documentation it asks for, in the right language, in a form that holds up later, is a larger one. That is the job we took.
